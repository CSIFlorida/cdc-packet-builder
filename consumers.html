Consumer Database

Keep each consumer's details — identity, address, guardian, Representative, consultant, budget, Cost Plan services and latest statement — in one profile. Update it whenever something changes; every section of the app can then fill itself from it.

  1. Add a consumer (or Add from saved applications).
  2. Fill in what you know and attach their documents.
  3. Click a Use this profile button, or choose the consumer from the list at the top of any section.

Your privacy

The database and documents are stored only in this browser on this computer — nothing is sent anywhere. Use Export backup to keep a copy or move it to another computer (the backup file contains private information: store it securely). Use Erase all data on this device on shared computers.
