Only items approved on the Support Plan / Cost Plan. OTEs are only equipment (DME), home modifications (ENV) and vehicle modifications (VMOD), entered as 1 unit. STEs: plan at least 92% of the approved quantity.

CDC+ Purchasing Plan

Once the participant is approved and has the Budget Authorization Form, the participant or CDC+ Representative writes a Purchasing Plan showing how the monthly budget will be used. This page fills APD's Purchasing Plan workbook (Version 3.0-C) for you.

  1. Basics: effective date (1st of a month), approved monthly budget, APD area, FFI, participant and Representative.
  2. Needs: copy the needs/goals and approved services from the Support Plan and Cost Plan; list what this plan buys.
  3. Services: every regular monthly provider. For each critical service, list at least two EBUs right below it — the app computes the EBU Added Cost that Excel's macro button normally does.
  4. Supplies, Cash, Savings, OTE/STE as needed. The right-hand panel keeps the budget balanced: Services + Cash + Savings must equal the budget, and Savings can't be negative.
  5. Download, review in Excel, print, number the pages, sign Page 6, and give it to your consultant with the provider packets of anyone new on the plan.
