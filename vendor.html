Welcome to the CDC+ Packet Builder

This tool helps CDC+ participants and representatives complete the Vendor / Independent Contractor packet without filling the same information into four different forms.

  1. Answer the questions in sections 1–7. Required items are marked with *. The panel on the right shows what is still missing.
  2. Attach the provider's Level 2 background screening letter and a voided check (or bank letter) if you have them — a photo or PDF is fine.
  3. Download the full packet (one PDF, in the order APD requires) or each form separately.
  4. Print, sign and date: the provider signs the W-9, the Good Moral Character certification and the Direct Deposit form. Keep a copy, then send the packet with your updated Purchasing Plan or Quick Update form to your CDC+ consultant.

Your privacy

Everything you type — including Social Security, tax ID and bank numbers — stays on this device, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere; the PDFs are created on your device. Use Export backup to keep a copy, and Erase all data on this device when you are done, especially on a shared or public computer.
