Direct-Hire Employee Packet

Use this for every employee on the Purchasing Plan, including Emergency Back Ups. Natural supports (unpaid volunteers) need only the Employee Information Form and a Level 2 clearance letter.

  1. Answer the questions in sections 1–10. Items marked * are required; the right-hand panel lists what is missing.
  2. Attach the Level 2 background screening clearance letter and a pre-printed voided check (handwritten checks are not accepted).
  3. Download the full packet or individual forms, then print, sign and date.
  4. I-9 Section 2: the participant or representative must look at the employee's original documents. A representative signs as “Representative Name for Consumer Name”. The first day of employment must be a future date from the signature.
  5. Send the packet with the updated Purchasing Plan or Quick Update to your CDC+ consultant. Keep copies.
