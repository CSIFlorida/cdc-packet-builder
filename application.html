Before you start

  • Fully enrolled on the APD iBudget Waiver (not pre-enrollment)
  • Lives in own or family home (not an APD group home)
  • Most recent approved Support Plan and Cost Plan (from the consultant)
  • CDC+ New Consumer/Representative training taken and Readiness Review passed (85%+)
  • CDC+ Consultant chosen

1Participant (Application Section I)

Legal name — no nicknames. Boxes on the Application are printed in CAPITAL letters automatically.

2Legal status & who signs (Section II)

Parent / legal guardian / legal representative — this person signs the application and enrollment forms. The CDC+ Representative cannot sign them unless they are also the guardian.

The IRS requires proof of this person's authority to sign for the participant (for example a birth certificate for a parent, guardianship papers or power of attorney). Attach it in section 9 — without it, Forms 2678 and 8821 are returned.

8Enrollment Packet details

Applying to CDC+

This page completes the documents a consumer sends to apply for the Consumer-Directed Care Plus program — the Application Packet and the Enrollment Packet — from one set of answers.

  1. Check the “Before you start” list and have the latest approved Support Plan and Cost Plan at hand.
  2. Answer sections 1–9. The right-hand panel shows what is missing.
  3. Download the full application, print it, then sign, date and initial where shown. The consumer signs — or the parent / legal guardian / legal representative. A CDC+ Representative cannot sign these forms unless they are also the guardian.
  4. Give the originals to the CDC+ Consultant (keep copies). Allow at least three weeks; CDC+ then sends a Budget Authorization Form.

The interview forms for choosing a consultant (Hiring and Working with Your Consultant, interview questions, New Consultant Evaluation) are not part of what is submitted, so they are not included.
