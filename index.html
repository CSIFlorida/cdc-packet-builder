How it works

  1. Choose what to complete and answer the questions. A participant's details entered once can be reused in the other packets.
  2. Attach the Level 2 background screening letter and a pre-printed voided check, if you have them.
  3. Download the complete packet as one PDF in APD's order, print, sign, and send it to your CDC+ consultant with the updated Purchasing Plan or Quick Update.

Your privacy

Everything you type stays on this device, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed. Each packet page has an Erase all data on this device button for shared computers.

Independent helper tool, not made, endorsed or reviewed by the Agency for Persons with Disabilities (APD), the CDC+ program, the IRS or USCIS. Always review every completed form before signing, and use the newest form version your consultant provides.
